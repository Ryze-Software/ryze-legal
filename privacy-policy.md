# Privacy Policy — DataPuller

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026
**Application:** DataPuller (internal QuickBooks Online integration)
**Operator:** Ryze Renal Care

## 1. Purpose of this application

DataPuller is an internal software integration operated by Ryze Renal Care. It connects Ryze Renal Care's own QuickBooks Online company files to Ryze Renal Care's internal business systems. It is not offered, sold, or licensed to any third party, and it is not listed on the Intuit App Store.

## 2. Data accessed

With authorization from a QuickBooks Online company administrator, DataPuller accesses accounting data stored in Ryze Renal Care's QuickBooks Online company files through the Intuit Accounting API. This may include customers, vendors, items, invoices, bills, purchase orders, payments, accounts, and reports.

All data accessed belongs to Ryze Renal Care. DataPuller does not access QuickBooks Online data belonging to any other business or individual.

## 3. How data is used

Data is used solely to support Ryze Renal Care's internal operations, such as reconciling inventory and purchasing records with accounting records and producing internal reports. Data is not used for advertising, profiling, resale, or any purpose unrelated to Ryze Renal Care's business operations.

## 4. Data sharing

DataPuller does not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks Online data with any third party. Data is not transmitted to any service other than Intuit's APIs and Ryze Renal Care's own internal systems.

## 5. Data storage and security

OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Intuit are stored on servers controlled by Ryze Renal Care, encrypted at rest, and accessible only to authorized IT staff. Tokens are never stored in browsers, shared drives, or messaging tools. All communication with Intuit's APIs uses HTTPS.

Accounting data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is stored only to the extent needed for the internal purposes described above and is protected by the same access controls that apply to Ryze Renal Care's other internal systems.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Retrieved data is retained in accordance with Ryze Renal Care's internal record-keeping policies. Authorization can be revoked at any time by a QuickBooks Online company administrator from the QuickBooks Online "Apps" settings or by contacting Ryze Renal Care IT, after which DataPuller will no longer access that company file and stored tokens for it will be deleted.

## 7. Children's data

DataPuller is a business-to-business internal tool and does not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18.

Ryze Renal Care may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to Ryze Renal Care IT at the contact address listed on the Ryze Renal Care website.
